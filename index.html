How we happened

The backseat of a car

You and two friends climbed into the back of my car — and somewhere in that ordinary moment, something in me quietly decided. I told myself it was a challenge. Looking back, I think it was just fate finding a very small door to walk through.

A broken leg, and you showing up anyway

I was laid up, useless, stuck at home — and you started talking to me every single day, just to check on me. I don't think you knew it then, but that's exactly when "friends" stopped being the right word for us.

Lathika,

Today is your birthday. For seven and a half years, I was the one standing beside you for this day — and this year, for reasons neither of us chose, I'm not. I won't pretend I've made peace with that. I just wanted you to have somewhere to see it, all of it, exactly as it happened.

Fate had its own plan for us, one I still don't fully understand. But nothing about what we had was a mistake — not the backseat of that car, not the days you called just to check on me, not the year you stood in a room I could barely breathe in. All of it was real. All of it mattered.

I can't be there today, and I can't be there for the years ahead the way I once thought I would. But I hope, wherever you are, you know you were loved completely — for seven and a half years, by someone who meant every bit of it.

Happy birthday, Lathika. Always.
